Block ads for your whole network with a $2 board

Pocket AdBlock turns a few-dollar ESP32 into a DNS ad blocker and Wake-on-LAN node. Every phone, PC and TV on your Wi-Fi gets fewer ads and trackers, with nothing to install on them.

What it does

Blocks ads and trackers

Answers ad and tracker lookups with nothing, for every device that uses it as DNS. Lists update themselves every few hours.

Catches hidden trackers

A site's own subdomain that is really an alias of a blocked tracker (CNAME cloaking) is blocked too.

Hard to bypass

Tells Firefox and iCloud Private Relay to use your network's DNS, and can block well-known encrypted-DNS servers.

SafeSearch for families

Optional: Google, Bing and YouTube are kept in safe mode on every device, even while ad blocking is paused.

Wakes your PCs from anywhere

Wake-on-LAN with online checks, from the web UI, Home Assistant or the Pocket Wake app, without opening any port.

Works with Home Assistant

Appears by itself over MQTT: a blocking switch, query statistics and a Wake button for each PC.

Never unreachable

If it can't reach your Wi-Fi, its own setup network comes back so you can always get in. Hold BOOT to bring it up by hand.

Updates safely

New lists are checked before they go live; new firmware rolls back by itself if it doesn't start up healthy.

Lite or Pro

Both are free and keep updating themselves. Pick in the installer; switch any time by installing the other one.

Lite every board

  • The full ad blocker: blocklists, answer cache, hidden-tracker detection, bypass protection, SafeSearch
  • Local names, device names, per-device rules, rate limit
  • Wake-on-LAN, MQTT and Home Assistant
  • Smallest memory use: runs on a $2 ESP32-C3

Pro boards with PSRAM

  • Everything in Lite
  • Encrypted DNS (DNS over TLS) to your upstream
  • Phone alerts and commands through ntfy or Telegram
  • Top domains and the last 4,096 queries on the overview

Phone apps

Pocket AdBlock app

Finds your boards on the Wi-Fi without knowing their address, remembers the login and opens their settings. Android (APK); iPhone by sideloading.

Get the app

Pocket Wake

One-tap Wake-on-LAN for your PCs from anywhere, through the board's MQTT bridge. Android app, and a web app for iPhone and any browser.
