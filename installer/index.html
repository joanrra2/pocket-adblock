Pocket AdBlock installer

Flash the Pocket AdBlock firmware from your browser. Works in Chrome and Edge on desktop (Web Serial).

Your browser can't do this. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page over HTTPS.

Not sure about octal flash? The module name ends in "V" (1.8 V), e.g. ESP32-S3-WROOM-2-N32R16V. A wrong choice simply won't boot; flash again with the other option.

  1. Plug the board in with a data USB cable and press Connect and install.
  2. Pick the serial port. If none shows up, hold BOOT while plugging the board in.
  3. After flashing, join the Wi-Fi Sinkhole-XXXX (password sinkhole-setup); the setup page opens by itself.
